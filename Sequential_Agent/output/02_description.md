**Headline**
Know Your Intake. Keep Your Water at Temperature.

**Description** (115 words)
The Smart Water Bottle brings your daily hydration into your phone-based routine. It tracks your daily water intake, so you can see how much you drink from morning to evening, and it sends hydration reminders to prompt you to drink through the day. When you connect it to your smartphone, tracking and reminders fit alongside the rest of your schedule. It also maintains water temperature for up to 12 hours, so the water you pour in can stay at the temperature it started at for a full workday or a long training session. Designed for office professionals who want a nudge between meetings and fitness enthusiasts who want to keep tabs on their daily intake.

**Benefits**
- See your day at a glance: the bottle tracks daily water intake, so you can see how much you drink each day.
- Stay on rhythm: hydration reminders prompt you to drink through the day, and the smartphone connection fits them into your routine.
- Water that holds its temperature: the bottle maintains water temperature for up to 12 hours, enough for a work day or a training session.
